# AI Enablement Playbook

A reusable framework for designing, delivering, and measuring organization-wide Generative AI adoption programs — built from real experience rolling out GenAI training to 70+ staff and 240+ end users across a multi-year-group organization.

This isn't a theoretical framework. It's the actual structure (generalized for reuse) behind a live AI enablement program: a needs assessment, a tiered curriculum, a facilitator's guide, and the adoption story that came out of it.

## Why this exists

Most organizations don't fail at AI adoption because the tools are bad — they fail because nobody designed the *path* from "aware this exists" to "uses it safely and well, every day." This playbook is that path, broken into reusable pieces so any L&D or enablement team can adapt it.

Read the full story in [`CASE-STUDY.md`](./CASE-STUDY.md).

## How to use this

1. Start with the needs assessment — don't skip it. A curriculum built without it trains the wrong tier at the wrong depth.
2. Adapt the tiered curriculum to your org's actual tools and policies — the structure is reusable, the content isn't one-size-fits-all.
3. Use the facilitator guide as a checklist before your first session, not just a read-once document.
